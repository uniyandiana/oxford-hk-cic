認識牛津郡 About Oxfordshire

What you should know before you settle

牛津郡唔止得牛津大學——呢度有唔同性格嘅市鎮、活躍嘅就業市場，同埋各有特色嘅社區。 我哋整理咗新來港人最常問嘅問題：邊區啱住？樓價幾錢？邊間學校好？搭車去邊度？
Oxfordshire is more than the University. It's a county of distinct towns, a strong job market, and neighbourhoods with very different characters. Here's what newcomers most often ask: which area to live in, house prices, schools, and how to get around.

ⓘ 以下數據截至2025年7月，僅供參考，實際情況請以官方最新資訊為準。
Figures below are accurate as of July 2025 for general reference only — always check official sources for the latest numbers.

01 · Where to Live

🗺️ 地區特色 Districts of Oxfordshire

Each town and neighbourhood has its own character and price point

Oxford City

市中心 City Centre

大學、書局、博物館集中地，步行可達大部分地方，但住屋選擇少而貴，多為公寓及學生宿舍。適合唔想開車、鍾意行得到市中心嘅人。
Colleges, shops and museums on your doorstep — very walkable, but limited and expensive housing, mostly flats.

East Oxford

Cowley & Headington

Cowley 有 BMW Mini 廠及活力街道（Cowley Road 多元文化餐廳）；Headington 近醫院（John Radcliffe）及 Oxford Brookes 大學，適合醫護及大學員工。
Cowley: BMW Mini plant and lively Cowley Road. Headington: near the hospitals and Brookes — popular with NHS and university staff.

North Oxford

Jericho, Summertown & North Oxford

綠樹成蔭、寧靜住宅區，鄰近 Cherwell School（受歡迎中學），樓價偏高，適合有子女嘅家庭。
Leafy, quiet residential streets near the well-regarded Cherwell School — but among the priciest areas.

West Oxford

Botley & Osney

近 Seacourt Park & Ride，交通方便，樓價相對市中心平，社區感強。
Close to Seacourt Park & Ride, good transport links, slightly more affordable with a strong community feel.

North of City

Kidlington

牛津郡最大嘅「村」，近 London Oxford Airport，屋苑較新、樓價較相宜，多家庭入住。
Oxfordshire's largest "village," near London Oxford Airport, with newer housing and relatively affordable prices.

Market Town

Abingdon-on-Thames

牛津以南嘅市鎮，河畔靚景，設施齊全（超市、學校），車程約20分鐘入牛津市中心。
A riverside market town south of Oxford with good amenities — about 20 minutes' drive into the city.

Market Town

Bicester

以 Bicester Village outlet 聞名，新樓盤多，Bicester Village 站直達 London Marylebone（約46分鐘），適合返倫敦返工嘅人。
Known for Bicester Village outlet shopping, lots of new-build homes, and fast trains to London Marylebone (~46 min) — popular with London commuters.

Market Town

Witney & West Oxfordshire

牛津以西嘅傳統市鎮，前首相 David Cameron 選區所在地，較寧靜，多家庭及退休人士。
A traditional market town west of Oxford — quieter, popular with families and retirees.

Science Vale

Didcot & Harwell

科研重鎮，Harwell Campus 有大量科學及生物科技公司，Didcot Parkway 有快車直達 London Paddington（約40分鐘），樓價相對相宜。
Home to the Harwell science campus. Didcot Parkway has fast trains to Paddington (~40 min) and relatively affordable housing.

04 · Education

🎓 學校 Schools

State, grammar-style and independent options for your children

ⓘ 牛津郡嘅公立中學制度屬非揀選性（non-selective comprehensive），並無官方「Grammar School」，「名校」多指受歡迎、學額爆滿或 Ofsted 評分高嘅學校。學位以住址（catchment area）為主要分配準則，建議提早查閱心儀地址所屬校網。
Oxfordshire's state secondary system is non-selective (comprehensive) — there are no official grammar schools in the county. "Popular" schools are usually those with strong Ofsted ratings or oversubscription. Places are largely allocated by catchment area, so check the catchment for any address before you commit.
受歡迎公立中學（部分例子）Popular State Secondaries (examples)
The Cherwell School（北牛津，長期獲 Outstanding 評級）、Wheatley Park School（東面）、Cheney School、Matthew Arnold School 等。學額競爭激烈，務必查閱最新 Ofsted 報告及入學條件。
The Cherwell School (North Oxford, long-standing Outstanding rating), Wheatley Park School (east of the city), Cheney School, Matthew Arnold School and others. Places are competitive — always check the latest Ofsted report and admissions criteria.
知名私立學校（部分例子）Well-known Independent Schools (examples)
Dragon School（Prep, 4–13歲）、Magdalen College School、St Edward's School (Teddies)、Oxford High School (GDST)、Headington School、Radley College。學費及入學試各異，需個別查詢。
Dragon School (prep, ages 4–13), Magdalen College School, St Edward's School ("Teddies"), Oxford High School (GDST), Headington School, Radley College. Fees and entrance requirements vary — enquire directly with each school.

06 · Getting Around

🚌 交通 Transport

Buses, taxis, trains, airport links and driving in the city

巴士車費 Bus Fares
英格蘭（倫敦除外）單程巴士車費上限為 £3（至2026年12月），牛津市內大部分短途車程實際收費更平。可用 Contactless、手機 App 或 Freeflow 拍卡，唔會被收多過上限。
Adult single bus fares are capped at £3 across England outside London (until Dec 2026) — most short Oxford journeys cost less. Tap in with contactless, the mobile app, or Freeflow; you'll never be charged more than the cap.
的士及召車 App Taxis & Ride-hailing Apps
牛津主要有 001 Taxis（本地最大車行，設專屬 App）同 Uber（已獲牌照喺牛津營運，App 內亦設 "Local Cab" 選項連結 001 Taxis）。
The main options are 001 Taxis (Oxford's largest operator, with its own app) and Uber (licensed to operate in Oxford — its app also offers a "Local Cab" option linking to 001 Taxis).
火車站 Train Stations
Oxford 站（市中心西面）：直達 London Paddington（約1小時）及 Birmingham New Street。Oxford Parkway（北面，Water Eaton）：Chiltern Railways 直達 London Marylebone（約1小時，通常較平）。另外 Bicester Village 站去 Marylebone 最快得約46分鐘，Didcot Parkway 去 Paddington 約40分鐘。
Oxford station (west of city centre): direct to London Paddington (~1hr) and Birmingham New Street. Oxford Parkway (north, Water Eaton): Chiltern Railways direct to London Marylebone (~1hr, often cheaper). Also: Bicester Village to Marylebone in as little as 46 min, and Didcot Parkway to Paddington in ~40 min.
機場交通 Airport Coaches
The Airline 提供牛津往返 Heathrow（約£25，70–90分鐘，24小時服務）及 Gatwick（約£45）嘅直達巴士，途經市中心同 Brookes 大學。另外牛津以北嘅 London Oxford Airport（Kidlington）主要提供少量商務及包機航班。
The Airline coach runs direct between Oxford and Heathrow (~£25, 70–90 min, 24hrs) and Gatwick (~£45), stopping in the city centre and near Brookes University. London Oxford Airport (Kidlington) mainly serves business/charter flights.
⚠️ 零排放區 Zero Emission Zone (ZEZ)
市中心試行區域，週一至日早上7時至晚上7時收費，按車輛排放級別分為 £2／£4／£10（不合規車輛於2025年8月起加倍至最高 £20）。零排放車輛（電動車）免費。
A pilot zone in the city centre, charging 7am–7pm daily based on vehicle emissions: £2 / £4 / £10 (non-compliant vehicles doubling to up to £20 from August 2025). Zero-emission (electric) vehicles are exempt.
⚠️ 交通閘 Traffic Filters (Bus Gates)
牛津六個地點設有攝影機執法嘅「交通閘」試驗計劃，冇實體閘門，限制時段內（部分全日7am–7pm，部分繁忙時段）冇許可證嘅私家車經過會被收費／罰款。試驗期最長18個月，連同6個月公眾諮詢，實施細節可能隨時間調整。
A camera-enforced (no physical barrier) trial at six locations restricting private cars without a permit during set hours (some 7am–7pm daily, others peak-only). The trial can run up to 18 months alongside a 6-month public consultation — details may change, so check official updates before you drive through the city.
