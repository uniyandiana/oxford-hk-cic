認識牛津郡 About Oxfordshire

What you should know before you settle

牛津郡不止有牛津大學——這裡有性格各異的市鎮、活躍的就業市場，以及各具特色的社區。 我們整理了新來港人士最常詢問的問題：哪個地區適合居住？樓價多少？哪間學校較好？如何乘車往返各地？
Oxfordshire is more than the University. It's a county of distinct towns, a strong job market, and neighbourhoods with very different characters. Here's what newcomers most often ask: which area to live in, house prices, schools, and how to get around.

01 · Where to Live

🗺️ 地區特色 Districts of Oxfordshire

Each town and neighbourhood has its own character and price point

Oxford City

市中心 City Centre

大學、書店及博物館集中之地，步行可達大部分地方，惟住屋選擇較少且昂貴，多為公寓及學生宿舍。適合不欲駕車、喜歡步行往返市中心的人士。
Colleges, shops and museums on your doorstep — very walkable, but limited and expensive housing, mostly flats.

East Oxford

Cowley & Headington

Cowley 設有 BMW Mini 廠房及充滿活力的街道（Cowley Road 多元文化餐廳林立）；Headington 鄰近醫院（John Radcliffe）及 Oxford Brookes 大學，適合醫護人員及大學員工居住。
Cowley: BMW Mini plant and lively Cowley Road. Headington: near the hospitals and Brookes — popular with NHS and university staff.

North Oxford

Jericho, Summertown & North Oxford

綠樹成蔭的寧靜住宅區，鄰近 Cherwell School（一所受歡迎的中學），樓價偏高，適合有子女的家庭。
Leafy, quiet residential streets near the well-regarded Cherwell School — but among the priciest areas.

West Oxford

Botley & Osney

鄰近 Seacourt Park & Ride，交通便利，樓價相對市中心較低，社區歸屬感較強。
Close to Seacourt Park & Ride, good transport links, slightly more affordable with a strong community feel.

North of City

Kidlington

牛津郡最大的「村」，鄰近 London Oxford Airport，屋苑較新、樓價較為相宜，多為家庭居住。
Oxfordshire's largest "village," near London Oxford Airport, with newer housing and relatively affordable prices.

牛津市南面 South of Oxford

Abingdon-on-Thames

河畔景致優美的成熟社區，設施齊全（超市、學校、運動中心），交通便利（車程約20分鐘可達牛津市中心），樓價相對合理。適合想兼顧生活質素與價格、且有家庭的人士。
A well-established riverside community with excellent amenities and good value. 20 minutes by car to the city centre, yet with peaceful surroundings and good schools nearby.

Cherwell 區 Bicester

Bicester

發展迅速的新興區域，新樓盤眾多且質素高，Bicester Village 大型購物中心聞名國際。最大優勢是鐵路便利（直達 London Marylebone 約46分鐘），吸引許多往返倫敦上班的專業人士及家庭。社區服務不斷完善。
Fast-growing area with modern new-build homes and quality infrastructure. Home to Bicester Village shopping centre. Excellent rail links to London (~46 min)—popular with London commuters. Community facilities are continually expanding.

West Oxfordshire 區

Witney & West Oxfordshire

充滿特色的傳統市鎮，環境寧靜優美，保留了英國鄉村的魅力。許多家庭及退休人士選擇此地，社區感強，生活節奏舒適。若喜歡比牛津市中心寧靜但又有便利生活設施的環境，是不錯的選擇。
Charming, traditional market towns that preserve English village character. Popular with families and retirees seeking a slower pace of life while maintaining good local services and community spirit.

Vale of White Horse 區

Didcot & Harwell

科技及生物科技產業集聚地，吸引許多科研專業人士。Didcot Parkway 火車站提供往 London Paddington 的快速連接（約40分鐘）。樓價相對合理，新興社區，適合尋求職業發展機會的人士。
A hub for science and biotech professionals, with strong employment prospects. Fast rail links to London (Paddington ~40 min). Relatively affordable housing in an expanding community—ideal for those seeking career growth in the tech sector.

Cherwell 區北面

Banbury

牛津郡最北端的市鎮，樓價全郡最相宜，適合預算有限的新移民及家庭。鐵路便利（直達 London Marylebone 約1小時），近年發展漸活躍。社區設施漸完善，是尋求平價與交通便利的好選擇。
The most affordable area in Oxfordshire—ideal for new arrivals and families on a tighter budget. Good rail links to London (~1hr). Convenient location with growing community amenities and ongoing development.

Cotswolds 地區

Cotswolds (Chipping Norton, Burford)

英國最美麗的鄉村地區之一，蜜糖色石屋聞名世界，田園風光迷人。若嚮往鄉村生活、喜歡步行散步及探索傳統村鎮，這裡是理想選擇。需注意交通依賴駕駛，樓價因景觀及旅遊地位而偏高。
One of England's most picturesque countryside regions, famous for honey-stone cottages and rolling villages. Perfect for those who love rural life, walking, and traditional charm. Note: a car is essential, and property prices reflect the tourism appeal.

04 · Education

🎓 學校 Schools

State, grammar-style and independent options for your children

ⓘ 牛津郡的公立中學制度屬非揀選性（non-selective comprehensive），並無官方「Grammar School」，「名校」多指受歡迎、學額爆滿或 Ofsted 評分較高的學校。學位主要按住址（catchment area）分配，建議提早查閱心儀地址所屬校網。
Oxfordshire's state secondary system is non-selective (comprehensive) — there are no official grammar schools in the county. "Popular" schools are usually those with strong Ofsted ratings or oversubscription. Places are largely allocated by catchment area, so check the catchment for any address before you commit.
受歡迎公立中學（部分例子）Popular State Secondaries (examples)
The Cherwell School（北牛津，長期獲 Outstanding 評級）、Wheatley Park School（東面）、Cheney School、Matthew Arnold School 等。學額競爭激烈，務必查閱最新的 Ofsted 報告及入學條件。
The Cherwell School (North Oxford, long-standing Outstanding rating), Wheatley Park School (east of the city), Cheney School, Matthew Arnold School and others. Places are competitive — always check the latest Ofsted report and admissions criteria.
知名私立學校（部分例子）Well-known Independent Schools (examples)
Dragon School（Prep, 4–13歲）、Magdalen College School、St Edward's School (Teddies)、Oxford High School (GDST)、Headington School、Radley College。各校學費及入學試要求不同，須個別查詢。
Dragon School (prep, ages 4–13), Magdalen College School, St Edward's School ("Teddies"), Oxford High School (GDST), Headington School, Radley College. Fees and entrance requirements vary — enquire directly with each school.

05 · Healthcare

🏥 主要醫院 NHS Hospitals

Oxford University Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust runs four main sites — here's what each one does

John Radcliffe Hospital (JR) — Headington
牛津最大的急症醫院，設有 A&E（急症室）、婦產科（Women's Centre）、兒童醫院（Oxford Children's Hospital）、眼科、心臟中心及大部分緊急住院服務。大部分急症及分娩個案均會轉介至此。
Oxford's main acute hospital — home to the A&E department, the Women's Centre (maternity), the Children's Hospital, the Eye Hospital and the Heart Centre. This is where most emergencies and births are handled.
Churchill Hospital — Headington
專科醫院，主要處理癌症（腫瘤科）、腎科、皮膚科及器官移植，並無設立 A&E。
A specialist hospital focused on cancer care (oncology), renal medicine, dermatology and transplant surgery. No A&E here.
Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre (NOC) — Headington
專科骨科及風濕科醫院，處理關節置換、脊椎及運動創傷等病症，並無設立 A&E。
Specialist orthopaedic and rheumatology hospital — joint replacements, spinal surgery and sports injuries. No A&E.
Horton General Hospital — Banbury
服務牛津郡北面（Banbury 及周邊地區）的地區醫院，設有小規模 A&E。注意：其婦產科自2016年起已由顧問級降格為助產士主導單位，高風險分娩個案須轉往 John Radcliffe 醫院。
Serves north Oxfordshire (Banbury area) with a smaller A&E. Note: its maternity unit has been midwife-led (not consultant-led) since 2016 — higher-risk births are referred to the John Radcliffe.
ⓘ 此外，Warneford Hospital（Headington）主要提供精神健康服務。所有 NHS 服務均須先登記 GP，詳情請參閱我們的 生活資源 Resources 頁面。
There's also the Warneford Hospital (Headington) for mental health services. All NHS care starts with GP registration — see our Resources page for details.

07 · Getting Around

🚌 交通 Transport

Buses, taxis, trains, airport links and driving in the city

巴士車費 Bus Fares
英格蘭（倫敦除外）單程巴士車費上限為 £3（至2026年12月），牛津市內大部分短途車程實際收費更為便宜。可使用 Contactless、手機 App 或 Freeflow 拍卡付款，不會被收取超過上限的費用。
Adult single bus fares are capped at £3 across England outside London (until Dec 2026) — most short Oxford journeys cost less. Tap in with contactless, the mobile app, or Freeflow; you'll never be charged more than the cap.
的士及召車 App Taxis & Ride-hailing Apps
牛津主要的士服務包括 001 Taxis（本地最大車行，設有專屬 App）、Royal Cars 及 Uber（已獲牌照於牛津營運，App 內亦設有 "Local Cab" 選項）。下載 App 後可輕鬆召車。
Main options include 001 Taxis (Oxford's largest, with its own app), Royal Cars, and Uber (licensed in Oxford, offering a "Local Cab" option). Download the apps for convenient booking.
火車站 Train Stations
Oxford 站（市中心西面）：直達 London Paddington（約1小時）及 Birmingham New Street。Oxford Parkway（北面，Water Eaton）：Chiltern Railways 直達 London Marylebone（約1小時，車費通常較便宜）。此外，Bicester Village 站前往 Marylebone 最快僅需約46分鐘，Didcot Parkway 前往 Paddington 則約需40分鐘。
Oxford station (west of city centre): direct to London Paddington (~1hr) and Birmingham New Street. Oxford Parkway (north, Water Eaton): Chiltern Railways direct to London Marylebone (~1hr, often cheaper). Also: Bicester Village to Marylebone in as little as 46 min, and Didcot Parkway to Paddington in ~40 min.
機場交通 Airport Coaches
The Airline 提供牛津往返 Heathrow（約£25，70–90分鐘，24小時服務）及 Gatwick（約£45）的直達巴士。牛津以北的 London Oxford Airport（Kidlington）主要提供商務及包機航班。

08 · Good to Know

💡 其他實用貼士 Good to Know

Small things that make a big difference when you first arrive

🌦️ 天氣 Weather
牛津屬溫帶海洋性氣候，全年溫和多雨，冬季（12–2月）平均約2–8°C，夏季（6–8月）約12–23°C，日夜溫差可以頗大。建議常備防水外套及多件可疊穿的衣物，毋須過於執著「換季」添置衣物。
Mild but often wet year-round. Winters (Dec–Feb) average 2–8°C, summers (Jun–Aug) 12–23°C, with big swings within a single day. A waterproof jacket and layers matter more than a strict "seasonal wardrobe."
🏘️ 市政稅 Council Tax
牛津郡 Band D 平均市政稅約為 £2,557/年（2025-26），高於英格蘭平均水平（£2,280）。稅款因地區及物業級別（Band A–H）而異，租樓時通常由租客負責繳交，簽租約前應先確認由誰負責繳付。查閱 Oxford City Council 官方網頁以了解具體金額。
Oxfordshire's average Band D council tax is ~£2,557/year (2025-26) — above the England average (£2,280). It varies by district and property band (A–H); when renting, confirm whether you or the landlord pays before signing. Visit the official council website to check specific amounts.
🚲 單車文化 Cycling Culture
牛津是全英騎單車人口比例最高的城市之一，市中心單車徑設施完善，不少居民以單車代步上學上班。建議購買品質較佳的 D 形鎖，因單車失竊在牛津頗為常見，泊車時應盡量鎖於官方單車架。
Oxford has one of the highest cycling rates in the UK, with good cycle infrastructure into the centre. Invest in a solid D-lock — bike theft is fairly common, so always lock to an official rack.
🎓 大學學期 University Terms & the Rental Market
牛津大學及 Oxford Brookes 的學期分為 Michaelmas（10月）、Hilary（1月）及 Trinity（4月）。每年9月新學年開學前，租盤競爭及交通均會特別繁忙，如情況許可，建議避開9月尋找住屋或搬遷。
University terms run Michaelmas (Oct), Hilary (Jan) and Trinity (Apr). Late August–September (student move-in) is the most competitive time for rentals and the busiest for traffic — avoid house-hunting or moving then if you can.
♻️ 垃圾及回收 Bins & Recycling
各區議會（Oxford City、Cherwell、Vale of White Horse 等）的垃圾收集安排各有不同，一般分為綠色（回收）、棕色／黑色（一般垃圾）以及花園垃圾（或需另外訂閱）。遷入新居後，應到所屬議會網站查閱及登記收集日期。
Bin collection rules vary by district council (Oxford City, Cherwell, Vale of White Horse, etc.) — typically green (recycling), brown/black (general waste), and a separate garden waste subscription. Check your council's website for your collection schedule when you move in.
🥟 中式雜貨及社群 Chinese Groceries & Community
牛津市中心設有亞洲超市（如 Seoul Plaza 及 Lung Wah Chong），可購買中式調味料及食材；週末亦有不同的華人教會及興趣小組聚會。如欲認識更多同路人，歡迎加入我們的 WhatsApp 群組。
Asian supermarkets in central Oxford (e.g. Seoul Plaza, Lung Wah Chong) stock Chinese ingredients and seasonings. There are also Chinese-language church and interest groups on weekends — join our WhatsApp group to connect with others.
