# About the event 

To celebrate Lunar New Year, Oxford Hongkongers CIC will be hosting a series of free cultural activities at Templars Square Shopping Centre. Through hands-on craft and calligraphy experiences, the activities aim to introduce participants of different ages and backgrounds to the cultural significance of Lunar New Year and Hong Kong traditions. 

The programme includes creative activities designed for both children and adults, such as Lunar New Year rubbing art and Chinese New Year calligraphy (Fai Chun). Participants will be able to engage with traditional festive practices in a relaxed and welcoming environment. The activities are family-friendly and open to local residents, families, and anyone interested in learning more about Chinese culture. 

The activities will operate on a drop-in basis. However, due to limited space and materials, advance registration is encouraged to help with planning and resource allocation.
