Settling into Oxfordshire — the essentials, in one place

初到牛津，總有大大小小的事情要處理。我們整理了移英港人最常需要的實用資訊與官方連結， 由簽證身份、醫療、住屋到子女教育，助你更快安頓下來。
Newly arrived in Oxford? We've gathered the practical information and official links Hongkongers most often need — from visas and healthcare to housing and schools — to help you settle in sooner.

ⓘ 以下資訊僅供參考，詳情及最新安排請以各官方網站為準。
The information below is for reference only — always check the official sites for the latest details.
