Oxford Hongkongers CIC is a community interest company supporting Hongkongers settling in Oxfordshire. In February 2026 we ran a Lunar New Year market at Templar Square with ten stalls, welcoming more than 200 visitors to our own stand alone. For 2027 we would like to bring the celebration into the city centre, on Lunar New Year's Day itself.

What the market brings The mix

  • Food vendorsHong Kong street food and festive baking
  • Craft & gift stallsIndependent makers and small brands
  • CalligraphyVisitors write and take home their own fai chun
  • Family activitiesGames, lucky draws and photo moments

What we handle Our commitment

  • Vendor recruitment and curation. We begin approaching traders early, drawing on our community network of Hong Kong food and craft businesses across Oxfordshire and beyond.
  • Marketing and audience. We promote through our own community channels, which reach Hongkonger households across Oxfordshire, alongside local press and partner organisations.
  • On-the-day coordination. Setup, stall allocation, stewarding and pack-down are managed by our volunteer team.
  • Community programming. Free, open activities that invite local residents in, not only our own community.

What we are asking of Gloucester Green

A designated area for the day, so the market reads as one coherent celebration rather than scattered stalls. We would like to open recruitment early to secure the best traders; where a pitch is not taken up by our vendors, your regular traders are very welcome to fill it.

We would be glad to come over and discuss layout, logistics and terms at a time that suits you, and to talk through what a community-led event of this scale might look like commercially.

Lunar New Year falls on a Saturday in 2027 — the strongest possible day for a city-centre market.

6 February 2027 · Year of the Goat
We would love to talk this through in person.

Happy to visit Gloucester Green at your convenience to walk the site, discuss a designated area and agree terms. Early planning means we can secure strong traders and promote the date well in advance.
